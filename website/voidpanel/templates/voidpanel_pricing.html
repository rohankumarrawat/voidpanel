Frequently Asked Questions

Is VoidPanel really free forever?
Yes — VoidPanel Free is open-source and free forever on unlimited servers. No license key, no expiry, no hidden charges. You only pay if you want Pro features.
What does the 30-day free trial include?
The trial gives you full VoidPanel Pro access on unlimited servers for 30 days — no credit card required. After 30 days, you can choose to subscribe ($9/server/month) or stay on Free.
Is the $9/server price per month?
Yes. Pro is $9 per server per month (or $6.75/server/month on annual billing — 25% savings). Each server you activate with a Pro license is counted separately.
Can I use my own branding on VoidPanel?
Yes — white-label branding is a Pro feature. You can set your own logo, company name, and custom domain so your clients never see "VoidPanel" branding.
Does VoidPanel work with WHMCS?
Yes — our WHMCS module (Pro only) auto-provisions, suspends, and terminates hosting accounts directly from WHMCS. Perfect for hosting resellers.
What Linux distros does VoidPanel support?
VoidPanel supports Ubuntu 20.04+, Debian 11+, and AlmaLinux 8+. It runs on any cloud (AWS, DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Vultr) or bare-metal server.
