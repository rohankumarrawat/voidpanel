How to get Google OAuth credentials in 2 minutes
1. Open Google Cloud Console and enable the Google Drive API.
2. Go to Credentials → Create Credentials → OAuth client ID (Application type: Web application).
3. In Authorized redirect URIs, add: https://{{ request.get_host }}/control/gdrive/callback/
4. Paste your Client ID and Client Secret above and click Authorize with Google Drive!
Tip: Create a folder in your Google Drive and share it with the service account email (with Editor permission).
